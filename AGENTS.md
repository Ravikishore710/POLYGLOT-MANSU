### 2.1 Verbatim Problem Statement & How We Exceed Every Clause

From [hackathon.lexsi.ai](https://hackathon.lexsi.ai/) (Track 2b, Problem #7):
> "7. Unlearning: Remove a specific capability or body of knowledge (hazardous knowledge, private data, copyrighted content) using forget and retain sets. Prove it is gone under paraphrased and adversarial prompts, and show general capability survives."

Why standard entries fail—and why **Polyglot-MANSU** wins unanimously:
1. **The Lexsi Failure Mode (Quantization Reversal & Behavioral Camouflage):** In [*Forgetting That Sticks: Quantization-Permanent Unlearning via Circuit Attribution*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15138) ([Hugging Face Paper 2605.15138](https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.15138)), Lexsi Labs showed that standard unlearning (`RMU`, `NPO`, `GradDiff`, `SimDPO`) diffuses tiny updates ($47\times\text{–}828\times$ smaller than NF4 quantization bin widths) across all parameters. Under 4-bit compression—how edge models like Tiny Aya are actually deployed—weights snap back to their pre-unlearned bins and resurrect forgotten knowledge, while full-precision checkpoints merely redirect surface refusals (`CAD ≈ 0`). Furthermore, [HF Paper 2605.15138](https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.15138) explicitly identifies **generalization beyond NF4 to INT4/GGUF/AWQ** and **distributed/polysemantic circuits** as open research gaps.
2. **The Cohere Failure Mode (Cross-Lingual & Code-Mixed Circuit Bypass):** [`CohereLabs/tiny-aya-fire`](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/tiny-aya-fire) and [`CohereLabs/tiny-aya-global`](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/tiny-aya-global) encode factual knowledge across 70+ languages inside a compact 3.35B, 36-layer `Cohere2ForCausalLM` transformer (interleaving 3 sliding-window 4,096 RoPE layers with every 4th global NoPE attention layer, 16 query heads, 4 KV heads, 2,048 hidden size, 262,144 vocab) ([Vultr Tiny Aya Architecture](https://docs.vultr.com/models/cohere-tiny-aya/tiny-aya-fire), [Tiny Aya Spec](https://huggingface.co/JuliaKreutzerCohere/tiny-aya-global-prompt-postexplain/commit/7b779bee5ea0722f0ba68e74e357d96d739e1f00)). Recent 2026 multilingual unlearning work ([arXiv:2609.05976](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05976v1), [arXiv:2605.14404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14404)) proves English unlearning only breaks English output projections while leaving shared multilingual factual hubs intact—allowing instant extraction via **Hindi, Telugu, Hinglish/Telugish code-switching, or Arabic**.
3. **The Boundary Collateral Damage Mode:** Aggressive unlearning destroys adjacent benign skills (e.g., forgetting cyber-exploit payloads breaks benign Python socket programming, or forgetting one employee's PII corrupts answers about colleagues), a flaw highlighted by [`WMDP++`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22527).

**Our Unified Solution (`Polyglot-MANSU`):**
We isolate the **Cross-Lingual Invariant Knowledge Circuit ($\mathcal{C}^*$)** across language families via `CircuitKIT`, constrain parameter updates strictly to $\mathcal{C}^*$, project gradients into the **Multilingual + Hard-Boundary Fisher Null Space**, scrub hidden representations via **Mid-Layer Latent Adversarial Perturbation** ([`AGTAO`, arXiv:2602.01703](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01703v1)), and enforce a **Multi-Grid 4-Bit Permanence Floor** covering both **NF4 (`bitsandbytes`)** and **Symmetric INT4 (`GGUF Q4_K_M` / group-quantized INT4)**.

### 2.2 Exact Mathematical Formulation (Implement Exactly, Zero Shortcuts)

Let $\theta_0 \in \mathbb{R}^d$ denote the reference model weights before unlearning, $\mathcal{D}_f = \bigcup_{l \in L} \mathcal{D}_f^{(l)}$ be the multilingual + code-mixed **Forget Set**, $\mathcal{D}_r = \bigcup_{l \in L} \mathcal{D}_r^{(l)}$ be the general multilingual **Retain Set**, and $\mathcal{D}_b = \bigcup_{l \in L} \mathcal{D}_b^{(l)}$ be the **Hard-Boundary Twin Retain Set** (benign queries sharing high lexical/topical overlap with $\mathcal{D}_f$).

#### Stage 1: Cross-Lingual Harmonic Circuit Localization ($\mathcal{C}^*$)
Using [`CircuitKIT`](https://circuitkit.lexsi.ai/) (`algorithm="eap-ig"`, `ig_steps=5`, `level="node"`), compute causal attribution scores $s_v^{(l)}(\theta_0)$ for every candidate node/module $v \in \mathcal{V}$ (attention head output projections `o_proj` and MLP projections `gate_proj`, `up_proj`, `down_proj` across all 36 layers of Tiny Aya, paying special diagnostic attention to global NoPE layers $\{3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35\}$) separately across language subsets $L_{\text{disc}} = \{\text{en}, \text{hi}, \text{te}, \text{hinglish}, \text{ar}\}$.

To reject language-specific surface token heads and isolate **language-agnostic conceptual storage nodes**, compute the **Cross-Lingual Harmonic Attribution Score**:
$$H_v(\theta_0) = \left( \frac{1}{|L_{\text{disc}}|} \sum_{l \in L_{\text{disc}}} \frac{1}{\tilde{s}_v^{(l)}(\theta_0) + \epsilon} \right)^{-1}, \quad \text{where } \tilde{s}_v^{(l)}(\theta_0) = \frac{|s_v^{(l)}(\theta_0)|}{\max_{u \in \mathcal{V}} |s_u^{(l)}(\theta_0)| + \epsilon}, \quad \epsilon = 10^{-6}$$
Select the minimal top-$k\%$ subgraph $\mathcal{C}^*$ (sweeping sparsity $k \in \{5\%, 10\%, 15\%, 25\%\}$) that maximizes causal necessity $z$-score on $\mathcal{D}_f$ while maintaining coherence $> 0.95$ on $\mathcal{D}_r \cup \mathcal{D}_b$ under `CircuitKIT`'s 6-pillar faithfulness suite. Let $M_{\mathcal{C}^*} \in \{0, 1\}^d$ be the binary parameter mask corresponding to $\mathcal{C}^*$.

#### Stage 2: Retain + Boundary Diagonal Fisher Preconditioning & Conflict Orthogonalization
Before optimization begins, compute a single pass of empirical diagonal Fisher sensitivities on $\mathcal{D}_{\text{protect}} = \mathcal{D}_r \cup \mathcal{D}_b$ restricted to $\mathcal{C}^*$:
$$F_i = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{D}_{\text{protect}}|} \sum_{(x, y) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{protect}}} \left( \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}}(x, y; \theta_0)}{\partial \theta_i} \right)^2 \quad \forall i \text{ s.t. } M_{\mathcal{C}^*, i} = 1$$
Define the **Fisher Null-Space Damping Preconditioner** ([MANSU, arXiv:2605.15138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15138)):
$$P_i = M_{\mathcal{C}^*, i} \cdot \mathbb{I}\!\left[F_i \le Q_{q}(F_{\mathcal{C}^*})\right] \cdot \left(1 + \frac{F_i}{\text{median}(F_{\mathcal{C}^*}) + \epsilon}\right)^{-\alpha_F}$$
where $Q_q(F_{\mathcal{C}^*})$ masks out the top $(1-q)$ fraction (default $q = 0.85$, protecting the $15\%$ highest-sensitivity retain weights inside $\mathcal{C}^*$) and $\alpha_F = 1.0$ smoothly damps borderline weights.

Furthermore, at every training step $t$, compute the masked forget gradient $g_f^{(t)} = P \odot \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{adv-forget}}(\theta_t)$ and masked retain+boundary gradient $g_r^{(t)} = P \odot \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{protect}}(\theta_t)$. Whenever $\langle g_f^{(t)}, g_r^{(t)} \rangle < 0$ (destructive interference), apply **Adaptive Gradient Orthogonalization** ([`AGTAO`, arXiv:2602.01703](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01703v1)):
$$g_{\text{total}}^{(t)} = \left( g_f^{(t)} - \frac{\langle g_f^{(t)}, g_r^{(t)} \rangle}{\|g_r^{(t)}\|_2^2 + \epsilon} g_r^{(t)} \right) + \lambda_{\text{ret}} g_r^{(t)}$$

#### Stage 3: Latent Adversarial Unlearning Objective (Defeating Prefill, Probes & Relearning)
Standard NPO only suppresses output logits on clean activations $h^{(\ell)}$, leaving residual-stream representations linearly decodable and easily revived by a 5-step LoRA attack. Choose the peak attribution hub layer $\ell_{\text{hub}} \in \{15, 19, 23\}$ from $\mathcal{C}^*$. For each forget batch $(x_f, y_f) \sim \mathcal{D}_f$:
1. **Inner Adversarial Latent Step (1-step PGD in residual stream, weights frozen):** Find bounded perturbation $\delta^* \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times d_{\text{model}}}$ ($\|\delta_t^*\|_2 \le \rho \|h_t^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})}\|_2$, default $\rho = 0.08$) that **maximizes** the model's log-likelihood of generating the forbidden answer $y_f$ ([`AGTAO`, arXiv:2602.01703](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01703v1)):
   $$\delta^* = \rho \|h^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})}\|_2 \frac{\nabla_\delta \log p_\theta(y_f \mid x_f, h^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})} + \delta)\big|_{\delta=0}}{\left\|\nabla_\delta \log p_\theta(y_f \mid x_f, h^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})} + \delta)\big|_{\delta=0}\right\|_2 + \epsilon}$$
2. **Outer Hybrid Forget Loss (Latent-Perturbed SimNPO + Representation Erasure):**
   Combine reference-anchored NPO / reference-free SimNPO ([arXiv:2410.07163](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.07163v1)) evaluated under the worst-case perturbed state $h^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})} + \delta^*$ with cosine representation decorrelation against $\theta_0$'s forget hidden state $h_{\text{ref}}^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})}$:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{adv-forget}}(\theta) = -\frac{2}{\beta} \log \sigma\!\left( -\frac{\beta}{|y_f|} \log \frac{p_\theta(y_f \mid x_f, h^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})} + \delta^*)}{p_{\theta_0}(y_f \mid x_f)} \right) + \lambda_{\text{rep}} \max\!\left(0, \cos\!\left(h_\theta^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})}(x_f), h_{\theta_0}^{(\ell_{\text{hub}})}(x_f)\right)\right)^2$$
   while $\mathcal{L}_{\text{protect}}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}}(\mathcal{D}_r; \theta) + \lambda_b \mathcal{L}_{\text{KL}}(\mathcal{D}_b; \theta \parallel \theta_0)$ locks benign multilingual and boundary behavior to $\theta_0$.

#### Stage 4: Multi-Grid Quantization Permanence Floor (NF4 + Symmetric INT4/GGUF Survival)
To guarantee **zero quantization reversal** ($\Delta_{\text{PTQ}} \le 0$) across both `bitsandbytes` 4-bit NormalFloat (`NF4`, blocksize 64) and symmetric 4-bit group quantization (`INT4` / `GGUF Q4_K_M`, group size 32), compute for each active weight block $B$ containing parameter $i \in \mathcal{C}^*$:
* $\Delta_B^{\text{NF4}} = \left(\max_{j \in B} |\theta_{0,j}|\right) \cdot \max_{m} |q_{m+1}^{\text{NF4}} - q_m^{\text{NF4}}|$, where $\{q_m^{\text{NF4}}\}_{m=1}^{16}$ are the exact 16 `bitsandbytes` NF4 quantiles (`max gap ≈ 0.2774`).
* $\Delta_B^{\text{INT4}} = \frac{2 \max_{j \in B'} |\theta_{0,j}|}{15}$ over 32-element group $B'$.
* **Unified Bin-Clearing Floor:** $\tau_i = \gamma \cdot \max\!\left(\Delta_{B(i)}^{\text{NF4}}, \Delta_{B'(i)}^{\text{INT4}}\right)$ with margin $\gamma = 1.25$.

During the final $20\%$ of unlearning steps (Quantization-Aware Consolidation Phase):
1. Apply a **Straight-Through Estimator (STE) Fake-Quantization Hook** alternating between NF4 and INT4 simulated quantization in the forward pass on active $\mathcal{C}^*$ tensors so the optimizer directly sees post-quantization logits!
2. At consolidation checkpointing, for active Fisher-safe parameters ($P_i > 0$) whose accumulated displacement $\delta \theta_i = \theta_i - \theta_{0,i}$ satisfies $\eta_{\text{active}} \tau_i \le |\delta \theta_i| < \tau_i$ (meaning the optimizer moved the weight intentionally in a consistent direction, $\eta_{\text{active}} = 0.35$, but fell inside the quantization dead-zone), snap $\theta_i \leftarrow \theta_{0,i} + \text{sign}(\delta \theta_i) \cdot \tau_i$, followed by 15 steps of retain-only KL micro-calibration outside the dead-zone. Verify explicitly via unit test that `quantize_nf4(theta_final) != quantize_nf4(theta_0)` on $\mathcal{C}^*$ and $\Delta_{\text{PTQ}} \le 0$!
